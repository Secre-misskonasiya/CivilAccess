What is CivilAccess

A smarter way to manage barangay operations

CivilAccess is a web-based Barangay Management System designed to digitize and streamline the day-to-day operations of Barangay San Sebastian. It provides barangay officials with a centralized platform to manage residents, process requests, and maintain records with ease.

Built for local governance, the system reduces paperwork, minimizes processing time, and ensures that essential community services are just a few clicks away—both for administrators and the residents they serve.

What We Offer

Key Features of the System

Everything barangay officials need to serve residents faster, more accurately, and with full accountability.

Resident Management

Maintain a comprehensive and searchable database of all registered residents, including household information and contact details.

Document Request Processing

Allow residents to request barangay clearances, certificates of residency, and other official documents with automated tracking.

Blotter & Incident Records

Log, track, and manage incident reports and blotter entries with status updates to ensure timely resolution of community concerns.

Announcements & Notices

Publish and manage community announcements, health advisories, and barangay events to keep all residents well-informed.

AI-Assisted Program Recommendations & Planner

Leverage AI to suggest community programs, plan barangay initiatives, and identify resident needs based on local data and trends.

Role-Based Access Control

Secure the system with defined roles for Barangay Captains, Secretaries, and Treasurers, with appropriate access levels.
